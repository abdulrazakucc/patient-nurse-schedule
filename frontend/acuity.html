Nurse skills classifier

Dr. Waseem Altaf's acuity tool and nursing levels of care. Scheduling uses them to set each infant's nurse:patient ratio and the minimum experience level of the nurse assigned.

How Scheduling applies it. For each body system, the most intensive finding that applies is recorded. The most intensive column across all systems sets the ratio: intensive care is 1:2, or 1:1 for the footnoted criteria; where a column gives a range, the more protective end is used. The highest level-of-care criterion met sets the care level, N1 to N4. The minimum nurse level is whichever is higher — the column's, or the care level's (N1 Novice, N2 Competent, N3 Proficient, N4 Expert).
