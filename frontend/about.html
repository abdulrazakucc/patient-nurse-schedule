About the model

How NeoStay turns aggregated outcomes into bedside intelligence

Data foundation

Built on aggregated 2016–2026 outcomes for Very Low Birth Weight infants - median and interquartile length of stay, disposition and survival - stratified across 10 birth-weight bands and gestational age week by week.

How predictions work

Inputs are matched to the relevant weight and gestational-age bins, then linearly interpolated between neighbouring bins so each gram and week matters. Weight- and GA-based estimates are blended; disposition and survival derive from empirical case counts.

Admission condition

Admission condition (stable → critical) and respiratory support (room air → ventilator) raise the modelled acuity, sharpening the nursing forecast beyond weight and gestational age alone.

Staffing intelligence

Nurse workload follows AAP / AWHONN acuity-based nurse-to-patient ratios, modelling the stay as intensive (1:1), intermediate (1:2) and convalescent (1:3) phases to project nurse-hours, NHPPD and shift demand.

Unit scheduling

The scheduling view aggregates a live census into an immediate acuity-based demand, recommending bedside nurses plus a charge nurse per shift and total daily nurse-shifts.

Safe by design

A decision-support prototype - not a substitute for clinical judgment. Every figure is traceable to the underlying dataset and runs entirely on local infrastructure.
