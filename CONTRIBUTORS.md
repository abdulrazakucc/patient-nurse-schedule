# Contributors

NeoStay: Infant NICU Outcome and Staffing Intelligence is a research and
decision-support prototype. It is developed under two leadership roles with
separate areas of responsibility.

## Project and Clinical Lead — Waseem Altaf, MD, Neonatologist

Responsible for:

- clinical direction and clinical requirements
- interpretation of neonatal outcomes
- data stewardship
- domain validation
- hospital coordination

## Technical Lead, Lead Developer and Applied AI/Data Science Lead — Abdul Razak, PhD

Responsible for:

- application architecture
- analytical and algorithmic design
- outcome-prediction implementation
- acuity-based staffing and scheduling methods
- interactive analytics
- software validation
- full-stack development
- documentation and deployment engineering

## Division of responsibility

- **Clinical conclusions remain under clinical leadership.** The interpretation
  of neonatal outcomes, the clinical content NeoStay draws on, and any clinical
  conclusion drawn from it are the responsibility of the Project and Clinical Lead.
- **Technical architecture and software methodology are led by Abdul Razak.**
  Application architecture, analytical and algorithmic methods, their
  implementation, and software testing and validation are the responsibility of
  the Technical Lead.
- The technical role does not include providing clinical care, medical judgment
  or independent clinical validation.
- Software validation means checking that the software behaves as designed. It
  is not clinical validation. NeoStay is not a medical device and must never
  replace clinical judgment.

## AI-assisted development tools

AI-assisted development tools may be used to help write code and documentation.
Responsibility does not pass to those tools: architecture, design decisions, code
review, testing and software validation remain the responsibility of the Technical
Lead, and clinical content and conclusions remain the responsibility of the
Project and Clinical Lead.
